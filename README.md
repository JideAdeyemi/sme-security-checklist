# SME Security Checklist

A plain-English cybersecurity checklist for small business owners who don't have an IT team or a security specialist.

## Who this is for

Owner-managers of small UK businesses who are already juggling payroll, suppliers and day-to-day operations, and need a short list of actions that matter most.

## The checklist

- [ ] **Turn on multi-factor authentication** for email, banking and cloud accounts
- [ ] **Back up important files** and test that you can restore them (use 3-2-1-0 back up rule)
- [ ] **Keep software and devices updated**, including phones and routers
- [ ] **Use a password manager** instead of reusing passwords
- [ ] **Know how to spot phishing**, and agree on what staff should do if they click a phishing link
- [ ] **Limit who has admin access** to systems and accounts
- [ ] **Write down who to call** if something goes wrong

## Where to go next

- [NCSC Small Business Guide](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/small-business-guide)
- [Cyber Essentials](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/cyberessentials/overview)

## What this leaves out

This is a starting point, not a full security programme. It doesn't cover regulated sectors, specialist tools or incident response in depth.
